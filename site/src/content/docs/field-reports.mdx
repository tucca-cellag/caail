Recurring institutional state-of-field surveys: the annual landscape reports that track cellular
agriculture and alternative proteins as a sector, grouped by series so each year's edition sits with
its predecessors. The most recent edition of every series is marked **Current**; earlier editions are
marked **Superseded** and link forward to the current one, so a reader (or an agent) reaches the latest
figures rather than a stale baseline. The canonical list is
[`FieldReports.md`](https://github.com/tucca-cellag/caail/blob/main/FieldReports.md).
