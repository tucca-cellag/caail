**CAAIL** (the Cellular Agriculture AI Library) is a curated, openly licensed library of the work
where **cellular agriculture** and **artificial intelligence** meet: the papers and preprints,
software tools, databases and per-species datasets the field uses.

CAAIL records what has been researched and takes no position on whether a given approach works.
A research area is a rule for which work belongs in it, not a claim that the approach succeeds.

## How it is organized

- **[Papers Explorer](/papers/explorer/)**: primary research arranged as a matrix of AI method
  against research area. Each cell links to numbered references, with each reference's DOI where it
  has one and any associated code or data.
- **[Reviews & Reference Works](/papers/reviews/)**: reviews, perspectives and reference works,
  which sit outside the matrix.
- **[Software](/software/)** and **[Databases](/databases/)**: tools and lookup resources, grouped
  by application area.
- **Datasets**: data you would train or benchmark a model on, organized by species (cow, pig,
  chicken, fish and more), with cross-species reference data and AI/ML benchmarks.
- **Research Areas**: one page per column of the matrix.
- **Methods**: one page per row of the matrix, the AI/ML methods themselves.
- **[Talks & Videos](/talks/)**: lectures and explainers.

The site is a **navigation layer** over the library's files on GitHub, which remain the source of truth,
and every entry links to its own source. The summaries here are short by design, so follow the
links for schemas, licenses and full detail.

## Contributing

Suggestions for new papers, tools, datasets, and resources are welcome. See
**[Contributing](/contributing/)** for how entries are categorized and added, or open an
issue or pull request on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/tucca-cellag/caail).

## How to cite

CAAIL is archived on Zenodo with a citable DOI
([10.5281/zenodo.20295590](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20295590)). If you reference the library
or build on its curated content, see **[How to Cite](/cite/)** for the APA and BibTeX forms.
When citing a specific catalogued paper, tool, or dataset, cite the original source directly rather
than CAAIL.

## About TUCCA

CAAIL is a project of the **[Tufts University Center for Cellular Agriculture
(TUCCA)](https://cellularagriculture.tufts.edu/)**, an interdisciplinary center of excellence that
works to *"democratize the scientific building blocks of cultivated meat, build a pipeline of talent
and expertise, and foster public trust in this novel technology."* Its vision is *"a world where
everything from meat to milk to eggs can be grown from cells instead of animals."*

TUCCA's members come from five Tufts schools and eleven departments, and Tufts offers an
undergraduate minor in cellular agriculture. TUCCA's
[research](https://cellularagriculture.tufts.edu/research) runs across the field: cells, media,
scaffolding, bioreactors and scale-up, media recovery, life-cycle and techno-economic analysis,
consumer assessment, and nutrition and safety. CAAIL grows out of TUCCA's **modeling and
informatics** work, which develops predictive models of growth substrates, media composition and cell
interactions, and applies omics to cell culture, differentiation and scale-up.

Learn more on the TUCCA [website](https://cellularagriculture.tufts.edu/),
[about page](https://cellularagriculture.tufts.edu/about), and
[mission, vision &amp; goals](https://cellularagriculture.tufts.edu/about/mission-vision-and-goals).
