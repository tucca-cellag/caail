CAAIL is a curated resource library published by the [Tufts University Center for Cellular
Agriculture](https://cellularagriculture.tufts.edu/) (TUCCA). It has no accounts, no sign-up,
no newsletter, no forms, and no payments. You can read every page without telling us anything
about yourself, and there is nothing here to log into.

This page describes the little that is collected anyway, and why.

**Last updated:** July 2026

## Where this site is hosted

CAAIL is served as static files by GitHub Pages at `tucca-cellag.github.io/caail/`. Because that
is not a `tufts.edu` address, the site sits outside the scope of the
[Tufts University web privacy statement](https://www.tufts.edu/about/privacy), which covers
`tufts.edu` properties. This page fills that gap. If CAAIL later moves to a `tufts.edu` domain,
the Tufts statement will govern and this page will point there instead.

GitHub, as the host, receives your IP address and browser user agent in the ordinary course of
serving the page, as any web host does. That processing is covered by
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## What we measure

### Page views

We use **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to count page views and measure page load speed. It is
cookieless: it sets no cookie, writes nothing to your browser's storage, and does not fingerprint
your device. It has no persistent identifier for you, so it cannot recognise you across visits or
follow you to any other site.

What it records per page view is the page address, the referring page, your browser and operating
system name, page timing measurements, and the country your IP address resolves to. Cloudflare
states that it does not store any part of the IP address itself. It deliberately does not record
query strings, so anything after a `?` in a URL is discarded.

### Two events about what is useful

CAAIL exists to point at things, so the measurements worth having are about whether the pointing
works:

- **Outbound clicks.** When you follow a link to a paper, a code repository, or a database, we
  record the destination address and a coarse label for what kind of resource it is. This is how
  we learn which entries are worth their place in the library.
- **Site searches.** When you search CAAIL we record what you typed and how many results came
  back. Searches that return little or nothing are the most direct signal we get that something is
  missing from the collection, and they feed into what gets curated next.

The search box is the only place you can type free text into CAAIL, so it gets the most careful
handling in the code. We discard any query that contains an `@`, that is longer than 80
characters, or that is mostly digits, so a pasted email address, a block of pasted text, or an
identifier is never recorded. A query is recorded once, after you stop typing, rather than
keystroke by keystroke. The rules are in
[`analytics.ts`](https://github.com/tucca-cellag/caail/blob/main/site/src/lib/analytics.ts) if you
would rather read the code than take our word for it.

Neither event is tied to an identifier, and neither can be traced back to a person.

**Current status:** both events are built into the site but are not being sent anywhere yet, as no
analytics service is installed to receive them. They start recording only if CAAIL is added to
Tufts University's analytics, and this page will say so plainly when that happens. Everything else
described here is live today.

## What is stored in your browser

CAAIL saves three display preferences in your browser's local storage so the site looks the way
you left it:

| Key | What it remembers |
| --- | --- |
| `caail-nav-collapsed` | Whether you collapsed the left sidebar |
| `caail-toc-collapsed` | Whether you collapsed the "On this page" panel |
| `caail-tableview` | Whether you prefer data tables as tables or as cards |

These never leave your browser. Nothing reads them but the page you are on, and clearing your
browser storage resets them with no other effect.

## Why there is no cookie banner

The consent banners you see across the web come from the ePrivacy Directive, which requires
consent before a site stores information on your device or reads information already stored there.
Cookies are the usual way that happens, which is why the rule is often called "the cookie law."

CAAIL sets no cookies and no tracking storage of any kind. The three preferences above are stored
only because you asked for them by collapsing a panel or switching a view, which is the case the
rule exempts. So there is nothing here for you to consent to, and a banner would ask you a question
with no content.

We would rather not put a dialog in front of a reference library, and CAAIL is built to be readable
by AI research agents as well as people. A consent wall would block that audience for no privacy
benefit.

## Links to other sites

Nearly all of CAAIL is links out: to journal articles, DOIs, code repositories, sequence archives,
and databases run by other organisations. **Those sites are not ours and their tracking is not
ours.** Some of them, publisher platforms in particular, track considerably more than CAAIL does.
Following a link from here means leaving this policy behind and entering theirs.

Linked resources are independent of TUCCA and Tufts University and remain under their own licenses
and their own privacy terms. Listing something in CAAIL is not an endorsement of how its publisher
handles your data.

## Your choices

- **Block the analytics.** Any content blocker or privacy-focused browser will stop the Cloudflare
  beacon. Nothing on the site depends on it, and everything keeps working.
- **Send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.** Modern browsers can signal this automatically.
- **Clear the stored preferences** through your browser's site data settings.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have rights of access,
correction, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability over personal data about you. Given
that CAAIL holds no identifiers, we are very unlikely to hold anything that could be linked to
you, but you are welcome to ask.

## Questions

Privacy questions and data rights requests go to Tufts University's data privacy office at
**[dataprivacy@tufts.edu](mailto:dataprivacy@tufts.edu)**, the same address given in the
[Tufts web privacy statement](https://www.tufts.edu/about/privacy).

For anything about the library's content rather than your data, open an issue on
[the CAAIL repository](https://github.com/tucca-cellag/caail/issues).

## Changes

CAAIL is a small static site and this policy should change rarely. If we ever add analytics that
sets cookies or builds a persistent identifier, we will update this page and reassess whether
consent is required before making the change, not after.
