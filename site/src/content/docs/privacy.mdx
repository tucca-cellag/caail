**Effective date:** August 2026

This Privacy Statement describes how the Tufts University Center for Cellular Agriculture
("TUCCA", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares Personal Data in connection with the Cellular
Agriculture AI Library ("CAAIL" or "the Service").

Personal Data is information that is directly linked, or can be linked, to you.

## Scope

The Service is published as static files through GitHub Pages at `tucca-cellag.github.io/caail/`.
Because that is not a `tufts.edu` address, the Service falls outside the scope of the
[Tufts University web privacy statement](https://www.tufts.edu/about/privacy), which governs
`tufts.edu` properties. This Statement governs the Service.

The Service requires no account, no registration, and no payment. You may read every page without
providing Personal Data.

## Personal Data We Collect

**Data collected automatically when you visit.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page
views and page performance. For each page view we receive the page address, the referring page,
your browser and operating system, page timing measurements, and the country to which your IP
address resolves. Cloudflare states that it does not store any portion of the IP address, and
query strings are discarded. This measurement is in operation today, and TUCCA accesses the
resulting reports.

**Data collected by our hosting provider.** GitHub receives your IP address and user agent in the
ordinary course of serving the pages, as any web host does. That processing is governed by
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

**Data you provide voluntarily.** Where you submit a question through the optional "Ask CAAIL"
feature, we collect the text you submit. See
[Sharing of Personal Data](#sharing-of-personal-data).

**Usage measurements.** We record two events concerning your use of the Service:

- **Outbound link clicks.** Where you follow a link to an external resource, we record the
  destination address, its domain, and a coarse classification of the resource as a DOI, a code
  repository, or other.
- **Site searches.** Where you search the Service, we record the term you entered and the number of
  results returned.

Search text is the only free text collected. It is redacted before transmission, and again on
receipt: any query containing an `@`, exceeding 80 characters, shorter than two characters, or
consisting of seven or more digits with no letters is discarded and never recorded. The digit rule is set to catch telephone
numbers and similar identifiers while preserving accession numbers and DOIs, which are among the
most useful things to know people are searching for. A query is recorded once, after you stop
typing, rather than keystroke by keystroke.

These events are transmitted to an endpoint operated by TUCCA and are stored in aggregate. No
cookie is set, no identifier is assigned, and no part of your IP address is stored alongside either
event, so neither event can be attributed to you or linked across visits.

The endpoint does read your IP address when it receives an event, in order to count recent requests
from the same source and refuse a sender that is flooding the measurements with fabricated events.
For IPv6 the source counted is your network prefix rather than your full address. That count is
maintained by Cloudflare for the length of a short counting window, is never written to the event
records, and is used for no other purpose.

We do not set cookies, employ device fingerprinting, or assign any persistent identifier. We are
therefore unable to recognise you across visits or across sites, and we do not attempt to.

## Processing Purposes: How We Use Your Personal Data

We process the data described above for the following purposes only:

- to measure use of the Service and understand which resources are of value to readers;
- to maintain and improve the accuracy and coverage of the library's contents;
- to respond to questions submitted through the "Ask CAAIL" feature;
- to maintain the security and availability of the Service.

We do not sell Personal Data, use it for advertising, or use it to build a profile of you.

## Sharing of Personal Data

**Subprocessors and service providers.** We use the following vendors to provide the Service on our
behalf:

| Recipient | Purpose | Data received |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GitHub, Inc. | Hosting | IP address, user agent |
| Cloudflare, Inc. | Analytics, and hosting of the events endpoint operated by TUCCA | Page view data, the two usage events described above, and your IP address as the sender of an event |
| Sevalla | Hosting of the "Ask CAAIL" service (United States region) | The text of your question |
| Google LLC | Generative Language API (Gemini models), used to compose answers | The text of your question, with relevant passages from CAAIL |

**The "Ask CAAIL" feature.** This feature is optional and transmits nothing until you use it. No
request is made when a page loads or when the panel is opened. Where you submit a question, the
text you submit is transmitted to the recipients identified above, both of which are outside Tufts
University. The service records the full text of each question it receives in its server logs. We
are not presently able to state the retention period applicable to those logs.

**You should not submit personal, confidential, or identifying information through this feature.**
It is provided to answer questions concerning cellular agriculture and artificial intelligence
research, and you should treat it as correspondence with an external company.

The feature retains no conversation history, stores nothing in your browser, and requires no
account. It may be hidden for the remainder of your visit, and the Service functions without it.

**Other disclosures.** We may disclose Personal Data where required by law or where necessary to
protect the rights, property, or safety of TUCCA, Tufts University, or others.

## Our Use of Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Service sets no cookies and employs no tracking technologies.

The Service stores the following display preferences in your browser. These are set only where you
have expressly requested the corresponding behaviour, and fall within the exemption in Article 5(3)
of the ePrivacy Directive for storage strictly necessary to provide a service explicitly requested
by the user. They are not transmitted to us or to any third party.

| Key | Purpose | Storage | Retention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `caail-nav-collapsed` | Records that you collapsed the sidebar | Local | Until cleared |
| `caail-toc-collapsed` | Records that you collapsed the "On this page" panel | Local | Until cleared |
| `caail-tableview` | Records your table or card display preference | Local | Until cleared |
| `caail-chat-dismissed` | Records that you hid the "Ask CAAIL" button | Session | End of session |

Because no consent is required for the foregoing, no consent banner is presented. Should we
introduce any technology requiring consent, we will update this Statement and obtain consent before
that technology is deployed.

## Links to Third-Party Sites

The Service consists substantially of links to external resources, including journal articles,
code repositories, sequence archives, and databases operated by third parties. Those sites are not
operated by TUCCA and are not governed by this Statement. Their operators may collect considerably
more Personal Data than the Service does. Inclusion of a resource in the library does not
constitute an endorsement of its operator's privacy practices.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of,
erasure of, or restriction of processing of Personal Data concerning you, to object to processing,
and to data portability. Where processing is based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw
that consent at any time.

Because the Service assigns no identifier to you, we ordinarily hold no Personal Data capable of
being linked to you. The exception is text you have submitted through the "Ask CAAIL" feature,
which will appear in that service's logs.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to lodge a
complaint with your national supervisory authority.

## Security and Retention

The Service is a static site and stores no data of its own. Data held by the recipients identified
above is retained in accordance with their respective terms. We are not presently able to state
the retention period applicable to "Ask CAAIL" logs, and are seeking to establish it.

## Changes to This Privacy Statement

We may revise this Statement from time to time. Where a revision materially expands the categories
of Personal Data collected or the recipients with whom it is shared, we will make the change
prospectively and update the effective date above.

## Contact Us

Privacy enquiries and requests to exercise the rights described above should be directed to the
Tufts University data privacy office at
**[dataprivacy@tufts.edu](mailto:dataprivacy@tufts.edu)**.

Enquiries concerning the contents of the library, rather than Personal Data, may be raised on
[the CAAIL repository](https://github.com/tucca-cellag/caail/issues).
