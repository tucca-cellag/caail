# Field Reports

Recurring institutional surveys of where cellular agriculture actually stands: what is currently blocking the sector and where effort and investment are going, rather than what any single method can do. Produced by institutes and research groups on a repeating cadence rather than published as peer-reviewed research. Each report states its own scope and method, and those scopes differ, so read one before citing a figure from it.

A recurring report line is one series with many editions, listed newest first; when citing a figure, use the current edition, since the earlier ones are kept for historical trend lines. Stand-alone analyses that do not (yet) recur are listed first, on their own.

### [Rethink Priorities: AI and Cultivated Meat 2026](https://rethinkpriorities.org/research-area/ai-and-cultivated-meat/)

*Edition 2026, published 2026-06.*

Rethink Priorities' assessment (Moulange & McAuliffe) of what near-term AI can and cannot do for cultivated meat, weighed across five bottleneck categories: technical, economic, regulatory and political, consumer, and capital. It concludes that open-access data rather than model capability is the binding constraint on what AI can do for the sector, and that as AI compresses technical timelines the non-AI institutional work may become the more neglected. Explicitly not a systematic review, and its bottleneck list is not exhaustive. A stand-alone analysis rather than a recurring series.
