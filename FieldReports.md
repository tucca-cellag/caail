# Field Reports

Recurring institutional surveys of where cellular agriculture actually stands: what is currently blocking the sector and where effort and investment are going, rather than what any single method can do. Produced by institutes and research groups on a repeating cadence rather than published as peer-reviewed research. Each report states its own scope and method, and those scopes differ, so read one before citing a figure from it.

A recurring report line is one series with many editions, listed newest first. When citing a figure, use the current edition; the earlier ones are kept for historical trend lines.

## GFI State of the Industry

The Good Food Institute's de-facto annual reports across the cultivated, fermentation, and plant-based tracks, with supplementary data (PDF). Each edition surveys sector progress: investment, company counts, scientific and commercial milestones, and the bottlenecks the field is working against.

### [GFI State of the Industry 2026](https://gfi.org/resource/state-of-the-industry-downloads/)

*Edition 2026, published 2026.*

The current edition, reporting the most recent year across the cultivated, fermentation, and plant-based tracks with the accompanying supplementary data.
