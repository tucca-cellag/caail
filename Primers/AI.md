# AI for Cell-Ag Researchers

Introductory resources on AI and machine learning, gathered for wet-lab researchers: biologists, biochemists, bioprocess engineers and cell-ag practitioners. The page lists playlists, courses and books on the methods catalogued in the library, from statistics and classical machine learning to deep learning, then links to the agentic-AI and foundation-model work that CAAIL indexes. Computer scientists learning the cell-ag side have a companion page, [Cellular Agriculture for AI researchers](./CellAg.md).

## Learn the fundamentals

Video playlists, courses and books that introduce statistics, machine learning and deep learning, listed here for readers coming from biology. Books come last.

* [AI Fundamentals](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOspHqNVtKADfxkuDuHduUkDExBpEt3DF): an IBM Technology playlist. Free.
* [Statistics Fundamentals](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLblh5JKOoLUK0FLuzwntyYI10UQFUhsY9): a StatQuest with Josh Starmer playlist giving a general overview of statistics, which also serves as a reference for statistical concepts. Free.
* [Machine Learning](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLblh5JKOoLUICTaGLRoHQDuF_7q2GfuJF): a StatQuest with Josh Starmer playlist on a broad range of machine-learning topics. Free.
* [Neural Networks / Deep Learning](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLblh5JKOoLUIxGDQs4LFFD--41Vzf-ME1): a StatQuest with Josh Starmer playlist that runs from neural-network fundamentals through the transformer architecture behind ChatGPT and other large language models. Free.
* [Neural networks](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDNU6R1_67000Dx_ZCJB-3pi): an animated 3Blue1Brown series that starts with "But what is a neural network?" and goes on to backpropagation, transformers and attention. Free.
* [AI Models Explained](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOspHqNVtKAC-FUNMq8qjYVw6_semZHw0): an IBM Technology playlist of explainers on AI models. Free.
* [AI Agents Explained](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOspHqNVtKAB6AzNie7BrFhbg4dv4Gfz8): an IBM Technology playlist. Papers on AI agents are indexed in the agent rows of CAAIL's Papers matrix. Free.
* [Stanford CS230: Deep Learning (Autumn 2025)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rNRRGdS0rBbXOUGA0wjdh1X): Stanford Online's recordings of the Autumn 2025 lectures by Andrew Ng and Kian Katanforoosh, with sessions such as "What's Going On Inside My Model?". Free.
* [Stanford CS230: Deep Learning (Autumn 2018)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rOABXSygHTsbvUz4G_YQhOb): the Autumn 2018 lectures of Andrew Ng's Stanford deep-learning course. Free.
* [Neural Networks: Zero to Hero](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLAqhIrjkxbuWI23v9cThsA9GvCAUhRvKZ): Andrej Karpathy's course on building neural networks and a small GPT from scratch in code, from backpropagation up to a working language model. Free.
* [MIT 6.S191: Introduction to Deep Learning](https://introtodeeplearning.com/): MIT's introductory deep-learning course, with lectures and code labs, updated each year. Free.

### Books

* [The StatQuest Illustrated Guide to Statistics](https://statquest.org/statquest-store/): an illustrated book by Josh Starmer that covers statistics up to advanced topics such as ANOVA and ANCOVA, with PyTorch and R tutorials. For sale as a paperback or a PDF.
* [The StatQuest Illustrated Guide to Machine Learning](https://statquest.org/statquest-store/): an illustrated book by Josh Starmer that goes from the basic concepts of machine learning through neural networks. For sale as a paperback or a PDF.
* [The StatQuest Illustrated Guide to Neural Networks and AI](https://statquest.org/statquest-store/): an illustrated book by Josh Starmer that runs from neural networks to the Transformers that power ChatGPT, with PyTorch tutorials. For sale as a paperback or a PDF.
* [An Introduction to Statistical Learning](https://www.statlearning.com/): a textbook by James, Witten, Hastie and co-authors, with an R edition (second edition, 2021) and a Python edition (2023). Each chapter ends with a lab in R or Python. Free PDFs on the book's site; print copies are sold.
* [Modern Statistics for Modern Biology](https://www.huber.embl.de/msmb/): a 2019 book by S. Holmes and W. Huber (Cambridge University Press), written for biologists, on the methods of modern computational statistics, with R and Bioconductor code. The online version is kept up to date. Free online under CC BY-NC-SA; print copies are sold.

## Go deeper

Talks, courses, books and CAAIL pages at a more advanced level.

* [AI Agents & Foundation Models for Biology talks](../Talks.md#ai-agents--foundation-models-for-biology): talks on agentic AI, foundation models and language-model-based scientific reasoning, many from the Broad Institute's MIA series.
* [AI Agents & Foundation Models hub](../AIAgentsFoundationModels.md): CAAIL's page connecting agent frameworks, single-cell foundation models and the virtual-cell initiative, among them Arc's State and Stack models, the open Virtual Cell Challenge and Cell2Sentence.
* [Curated bibliographies & awesome lists](../AwesomeLists.md): community-maintained indexes of the AI, single-cell and bioinformatics literature. On the site, each list's card shows its GitHub star count and last-updated date.
* [Stanford CS224N: NLP with Deep Learning (Spring 2024)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rOaMFbaqxPDoLWjDaRAdP9D): Stanford's NLP course, covering self-attention, Transformers and modern LLM architectures. Free.
* [Deep Learning for Molecules and Materials](https://dmol.pub/): an online book by A. D. White, written for chemistry and materials science students and assuming knowledge of Python. Free.

## Where AI shows up in CAAIL

* [Papers Explorer](../Papers.md): CAAIL's matrix of AI methods against research areas. Agentic and foundation-model work sits in the agent and foundation-model rows, and general-purpose AI tools not yet tied to a cell-ag result sit in the AI Tooling / Methodology column.
* [Artificial intelligence and machine learning applications for cultured meat](../Papers.md#132): a 2024 review of AI and ML applied to cultured meat, by Todhunter and colleagues in *Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence*. Open access (CC BY).
* [Artificial intelligence for food innovation](../Papers.md#39): a 2026 review of AI in food innovation, by Datta, Kuhl and 15 co-authors in *Nature Food*. Paywalled at the publisher; a repository copy is free.
* [Software](../Software.md): software tools for computational and AI work, open-source and proprietary, grouped by application area.
