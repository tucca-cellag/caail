# Cellular Agriculture for AI Researchers

Introductory resources on cellular agriculture, gathered for AI and machine-learning researchers who come to the field from computer science. The page lists talks, reading, courses and articles on the field, then links to the AI and cell-ag work catalogued in the rest of the library. Biologists learning the AI side have a companion page, [AI for cell-ag researchers](./AI.md).

## Field foundations

Talks and pages on what cellular agriculture is, the science behind it, the state of the industry and the people doing the work.

* [Introduction to Cellular Agriculture](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-0eap5cg6mE): a video from TUCCA, the Tufts University Center for Cellular Agriculture. Free.
* [What is Cellular Agriculture?](https://www.new-harvest.org/what-is-cellular-agriculture): New Harvest's explainer and working definition of the field. The page describes cellular agriculture as a term whose definition is still evolving. Free.
* [Fireside Chat: Bruce Friedrich and David Kaplan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=59NrhsHQT0U): a conversation at the 2026 Tufts Future Food Innovation Day between Bruce Friedrich, founder of the Good Food Institute (GFI), and David Kaplan, director of TUCCA. They discuss cultivated meat and other alternative proteins. Free.
* [2026 State of the Industry: Cultivated meat, seafood, and ingredients](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F1s702taM5o): a 2026 GFI talk. Free.
* [2026 State of the Industry: Fermentation for meat, seafood, eggs, dairy, and ingredients](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eCBclCUZOg0): a 2026 GFI talk. Free.
* [2026 State of the Industry: Plant-based meat, seafood, eggs, dairy, and ingredients](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OEuehZB7YVM): a 2026 GFI talk. Free.
* [Seminar Series: The Science of Alt. Protein](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLaLco7qZryP4wmZsG5nOS2yhOIw1U2DJZ): a GFI playlist from its monthly Science of Alt Protein seminar series, which addresses a technical audience and covers research developments in alternative protein. Free.
* [Field Reports](../FieldReports.md): CAAIL's page of recurring surveys of the sector, among them GFI's State of the Industry and State of Global Policy series, grouped by series with the newest edition first.
* [GFI State of the Industry report downloads](https://gfi.org/resource/state-of-the-industry-downloads/): the Good Food Institute's annual reports on the cultivated, fermentation and plant-based sectors, as PDFs. Free.

## Read and take a course

Books, courses and articles on the science of cellular agriculture and on the bioprocess engineering behind scale-up. Field reviews and perspectives come last.

* [MEAT](https://meatbook.org/): a book by GFI founder Bruce Friedrich, making the case for cellular agriculture and alternative proteins. Purchase required.
* [Foundations of Chemical and Biological Engineering I](https://eng.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Chemical_Engineering/Foundations_of_Chemical_and_Biological_Engineering_I_(Verret_Qiao_Barghout)): an open LibreTexts textbook by Verret, Qiao and Barghout of the University of British Columbia. Its chapters cover chemical processes and process diagrams, reaction chemistry, phase equilibrium, energy balances, unsteady-state operations and process control, process economics, green engineering and process safety. Free under CC BY-NC-SA.
* [Books and reference works](../ReferenceWorks.md): CAAIL's page for the *Cellular Agriculture* textbook edited by Fraser, Kaplan, Newman and Yada, and for the *Encyclopedia of Meat Sciences*, with a DOI index of the encyclopedia's cell-ag-relevant chapters.
* [The science of cultivated meat](https://gfi.org/science/the-science-of-cultivated-meat/): a GFI overview by E. Swartz and C. Bomkamp, with sections on cell lines, cell culture media, bioprocess design, scaffolding, end products, cost and environmental impact. The first five sections each link to a GFI deep dive. Free.
* [The Protein Transition: The science behind alternative proteins](https://gfi.org/resource/plant-based-and-cultivated-meat-online-course/): a GFI online course in five self-paced modules. Module 5, "What is cultivated meat?", covers the technology value chain, costs, environmental impacts, regulation, food safety and scale-up. Free to enroll.
* [Alternative protein teaching library](https://gfi.org/resource/teaching-library/): GFI's repository of curriculum materials from alternative protein courses around the world. Free.
* [Cellular Agriculture Courses (TUCCA)](https://sites.tufts.edu/cellagcourse/): TUCCA's open course-materials site, on the bioengineering behind cultivated meat, with materials shared for educators and students. Free.
* [Cellular Agriculture and Biofabricated Foods (BME 173)](https://sites.tufts.edu/cellagcourse/cellular-agriculture-and-biofabricated-foods-bme-173/): a TUCCA lecture course on the fundamentals of cellular agriculture and biofabricated foods. Course materials are free online.
* [Cellular Agriculture and Cultured Meat Lab (BME 174)](https://sites.tufts.edu/cellagcourse/cell-ag-and-cultured-meat-lab-bme-174/): a TUCCA lab course in which students isolate livestock cells, differentiate them into fat and muscle tissue, produce cultured meat and analyze the constructs. Course materials are free online.
* [Sustainable Materials (BME 0193)](https://sites.tufts.edu/cellagcourse/sustainable-materials-course-bme-0193/): a TUCCA course on the use of cellular agriculture in the materials industry. Course materials are free online.
* [TUCCA courses](https://cellularagriculture.tufts.edu/education/courses): the center's official course listing on its main site.
* [Cellular Agriculture (Advanced Course)](https://biotechdelft.com/courses/cellular-agriculture/): a Biotech Delft course on precision fermentation and cultured meat, covering bioprocessing and food product development. It is also an elective in the postgraduate programs of TU Delft. Course fee.
* [Cellular Bioprocess Technology Summer Course](https://cbt.cems.umn.edu/): an interactive four-day short course on cellular bioprocess technology at the University of Minnesota. In 2026 it offered an optional half-day add-on workshop on machine learning in cellular bioprocess technology. Course fee.

### Field reviews and perspectives

Review and perspective articles that survey the field, listed by year of publication. Those catalogued in CAAIL link to their entry in [Papers.md](../Papers.md), and the others link to their DOI. Access varies.

* [Scientific, sustainability and regulatory challenges of cultured meat](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43016-020-0112-z): by Post, Levenberg, Kaplan and six co-authors, in *Nature Food* (2020). Paywalled at the publisher; a repository copy is free.
* [Plant-based and cell-based approaches to meat production](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-20061-y): by Rubio, Xiang and Kaplan, in *Nature Communications* (2020). Open access (CC BY).
* [Cultured meat: Creative solutions for a cell biological problem](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcb.2022.10.002): by Stout, Kaplan and Flack, in *Trends in Cell Biology* (2023). Free to read at the publisher.
* [Stem cell-based strategies and challenges for production of cultivated meat](../Papers.md#246): by Jara, Park, Vahmani, Van Eenennaam, Smith and Denicol, in *Nature Food* (2023). Paywalled.
* [Meating the moment](../Papers.md#38): by McNulty, Stout and Kaplan, in *EMBO Reports* (2025). Open access (CC BY).

## See AI applied to cell-ag

Reviews, reports, talks and CAAIL pages on AI and computational modeling applied to cellular agriculture.

* [AI and cultivated meat: Near-term impacts of AI on the commercial viability of cultivated meat](https://rethinkpriorities.org/research-area/ai-and-cultivated-meat/): a 2026 Rethink Priorities report by H. Moulange and W. McAuliffe. It assesses, across several categories of bottleneck, where near-term AI can and cannot affect the commercial viability of cultivated meat. The authors conclude that open-access data, not model capability, is the binding constraint. Free.
* [Artificial intelligence and machine learning applications for cultured meat](../Papers.md#132): a review by Todhunter, Jubair, Verma, Saqe, Shen and Duffy (New Harvest), in *Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence* (2024). Open access (CC BY).
* [Artificial intelligence for food innovation](../Papers.md#39): a review by Datta, Kuhl and 15 co-authors, in *Nature Food* (2026). Paywalled at the publisher; a repository copy is free.
* [Cell growth modeling review and recommendations for cultivated meat production](https://gfi.org/resource/cell-growth-modeling-review-for-cultivated-meat-production/): a 2025 study by Frohlich, Harsini and Swartz, from GFI and BioFarm Designs. It reviews cell growth models for cultivated meat production and highlights gaps in the data. Free download.
* [Papers Explorer](../Papers.md): CAAIL's matrix of AI methods against research areas, with links to a paper's code and data where it has them.
* [Applied AI/ML for Cellular Agriculture talks](../Talks.md#applied-aiml-for-cellular-agriculture): conference and webinar talks on AI applied to specific cell-ag problems.
* [Research Areas](../ResearchAreas/MediaOptimization.md): CAAIL's deep-dive pages, one for each research area in the Papers matrix. The link opens the Media Optimization page.
